# GEO — AI-search discoverability and entity clarity

GEO here means making a real professional and his services straightforward to identify, quote and verify in general search and AI-assisted answers. There is **no guarantee of AI citations, traffic or placement**.

## Already implemented

- Explicit `Person` schema with name, professional focus, alumni university and Islamabad location.
- The same canonical name on all pages; separate recruiter and service intent pages.
- `Service` structured data tied back to the person entity.
- Public resume download, readable timeline and relevant software names.
- `llms.txt` optional text summary for AI-related crawlers, but **not an official ranking factor**.

## Entity and citation work to do with real evidence

1. Verify one public email address and consistent role description across the website, CV and any owned social profiles.
2. Add *verified, owned* LinkedIn, GitHub or professional identities with `sameAs` only after confirming URLs.
3. Use concise original explanations of work in real estate, healthcare records, education and project coordination; never publish patient/client private data.
4. Publish demonstrable projects, screenshots or anonymized workflows only when authorized, with methods and scope clearly described.
5. Build honest external citations (legitimate business directories, profiles and contributions), not mass-created spam listings.
6. Record what actual prospects and recruiters ask; improve content around those questions.
7. Check crawlability and structured data on real production URLs and evaluate referral traffic plus legitimate leads.
