FAQ

Straight answers

Can Adnan support North American teams remotely?

Yes. Adnan is based in Islamabad, Pakistan (UTC+5) and has spent multiple roles working US and Canadian business hours. Availability and hours overlap are confirmed directly before any engagement, as they depend on what the role requires.

How does a business schedule a discovery call?

Use the "Book a free discovery call" button anywhere on the site. You will answer a few short questions about what you need, leave your details, and pick a time. If no calendar is connected, your inquiry is saved and Adnan can follow up by email or, if you provide a number, by call or text — your request is not automatically booked.
